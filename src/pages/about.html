Our story

From a single dream to a one-stop destination.

Janeses began with a passion for cake baking and decoration. We first traded as Janeses Cakes & Bridal, and that craft, doing small things beautifully, is still how we work today.

Today, from our home at Sunny Peace Plaza on Zaria Road, Jos, we bring catering, cakes, bridal services, décor, drinks and event essentials together under one roof, so every celebration we touch feels considered, cohesive and calm to plan.

What hasn't changed is the promise we started with: precision, style and quality, from concept to execution.

Our Mission

To deliver elegant, stress-free and memorable event experiences through creativity, professionalism and excellence, handling every detail so our clients can simply enjoy their moment.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's most trusted one-stop event and lifestyle solutions brand, known as much for the quality of our work as the ease of working with us.

Meet the founder

Janet Olajumoke Aiyenigba

Founder & CEO, Janeses Integrated Services Ltd

Janet Olajumoke Aiyenigba is the founder of Janeses, a growing full-service creative and hospitality company built around the art of creating memorable experiences. It began with a passion for cake baking and decoration, trading first as Janeses Cakes & Bridal, and has grown into a multifaceted brand offering wedding planning and styling, bridal services, cakes, catering and professional training programs.

Through Janeses, Janet has combined creativity, attention to detail and a passion for excellence to serve clients while also equipping others with practical skills they can build careers and businesses around.

For Janet, Janeses is more than a business; it is a reflection of her passion for creativity, excellence and creating memorable experiences.
